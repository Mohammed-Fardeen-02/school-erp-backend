# Staff Module (`src/modules/staff/`)

## Domain Scope

Manages school personnel including teachers, administrative employees, non-teaching staff, and department assignments.

## Planned Responsibilities

- Staff biographical records, qualifications, and employment history
- Department and designation management
- Work allocation, subject teacher assignments, and homeroom teacher mapping
- Integration points with attendance and payroll modules
